THE PROBLEM

Painting has always been a word-of-mouth business. That's the gap.

India's real estate execution layer is highly fragmented. Developers and facility owners manage multiple buildings, tight handover deadlines, and demanding buyers.

Find a contractor, hope he's good, cross your fingers on the finish — that's how most painting and waterproofing gets bought in India today. PaintBharat exists because nobody has brought real accountability to this category at scale.

NON-CONSUMPTION

Unorganized contractors

Cheap, fast to engage, and structurally unable to offer a developer consistent quality across ten sites at once. PaintBharat competes on accountability, not price.

B2C, NOT B2B

Consumer painting apps

Built for a homeowner's single room, with one or two painters per job. Nobody has built the contractor-team, milestone-escrow layer a developer's procurement desk actually needs.

DATA MOAT

Regional specialists

Some claim long warranties, few can prove them. PaintBharat ties its warranty to photo, GPS and checklist data captured at every milestone — verifiable, not just advertised.

Questions

Straight answers, in case you're scanning.

What does PaintBharat do?+

PaintBharat is an engineering-led painting and waterproofing services company for India's real estate developers, commercial property owners, and housing societies. It standardizes execution across contractor teams and verifies every project against a documented quality checklist, backed by a data-supported workmanship warranty.

Does PaintBharat work with individual homeowners?+

PaintBharat is built B2B-first, serving developers, builders, commercial facility owners, and housing societies rather than individual retail homeowners — deliberately, since that's the segment least served by existing consumer painting platforms in India today.

Which cities does PaintBharat operate in?+

PaintBharat is launching in Pune, Maharashtra, with a phased expansion plan to additional Indian metros as its contractor network and verification systems scale.

How is this different from consumer apps like Urban Company?+

Consumer platforms are built for one homeowner and one or two painters per job. PaintBharat is built for multi-unit, multi-building B2B contracts — a contractor team, one accountable point of contact, milestone-based payment, and a verified quality record.

Is there a workmanship warranty?+

Yes — tied directly to the quality-verification data captured at project handover (photos, GPS, checklist sign-off), rather than a generic or verbal guarantee.

Use the Investor, Customer/Pilot, or Partner & Supplier inquiry forms above — each is routed to the relevant team.
